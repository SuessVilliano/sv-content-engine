The most profitable skill in trading cannot be learned from a chart.

Patience.

Waiting for the setup.
Waiting for confirmation.
Not forcing trades out of boredom or FOMO.

The traders who make the most money per trade...
often make the fewest trades.

Quality over quantity is not a cliche. It is a strategy.

One clean setup a week beats 30 mediocre ones every time.
Your job is not to be busy. It is to be precise.
