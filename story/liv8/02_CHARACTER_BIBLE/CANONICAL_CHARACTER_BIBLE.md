# Canonical Jamaur Character Bible

## Identity
Use the supplied photo as canonical facial reference.
Do not randomly alter facial geometry between shots.

### Preserve
- close-cropped hairstyle and hairline
- eyebrow shape
- warm brown skin tone
- narrow goatee/chin facial-hair pattern
- jaw proportions
- smile proportions
- ear placement
- lean facial structure

## Visual treatment
Cinematic graphic-novel realism.
Not cartoonish.
Not a superhero suit.
Not plastic AI-avatar skin.
Real human micro-expression, subtle texture, cinematic shadows.

## Modes

### Executive
Dark navy/black suit, white shirt, minimal accessories.
Finance, formal meetings, leadership, major turning points.

### Builder
Minimal black technical clothing or fitted hoodie.
Late-night screens, coding, AI, workflow design.

### Trader
Relaxed dark clothing, controlled posture, multi-monitor environment.
Avoid casino iconography.

### Creative
Studio, microphone, DAW, warm practical lights, music collaborators.

### Seeker
Neutral clothing, outdoors, sunrise/water/quiet room.
Philosophy, consciousness, spirituality.

### Father
Most naturalistic mode.
Warm light, ordinary clothing, slower camera, less stylization.

### Ghost
Underground years.
Silhouette, hoodie, car reflections, rearview mirror, low exposure.
Never criminal-coded; the intent is privacy and hyper-awareness.

### Future Self
Older but unmistakably the same person.
Calmer frame, fewer possessions, more natural light.
Represents a possible trajectory, not prophecy.

## Performance
Jamaur is:
- confident without constant swagger
- reflective without sounding defeated
- curious rather than omniscient
- emotionally controlled, not emotionally absent
- capable of humor but never undercutting grief
- willing to say “I don’t know”

## Character flaw rule
Do not make him always right.
Show overconfidence, misplaced trust, impatience, excessive ambition,
withdrawal, and the cost of learning.

## Superhuman rule
AI is not his superpower.
His superpower is synthesis.
AI amplifies it.
