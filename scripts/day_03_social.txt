Fear in trading is not your enemy. Ignored fear is.

The traders who succeed are not the ones who feel no fear.
They are the ones who use it as information.

Fear says: slow down. Check the setup. Is this within your rules?
It does not say: close your account and quit.

Most traders either ignore fear entirely (and blow up)...
or they listen too hard (and never pull the trigger).

The disciplined trader does neither.
They hear the fear. They ask what it is pointing at.
Then they decide based on the rules. Not the emotion.

Fear is a signal. Not a sentence.
