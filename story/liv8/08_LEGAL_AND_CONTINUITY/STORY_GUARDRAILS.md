# Story / Legal Guardrails

This project is based on lived experience but dramatized.

## Always distinguish
- documented fact
- personal recollection
- interpretation
- composite/dramatized imagery
- future imagination

## Avoid
- identifying private people unnecessarily
- accusing named people of crimes without documented public evidence
- depicting unverified motives as fact
- revealing details restricted by court order, NDA, gag order, settlement, or counsel
- recreating illegal drone methods or operational wrongdoing
- publishing private customer/client financial information
- guaranteeing investment, trading, insurance, software, or business outcomes

## Safer dramatic language
Use:
“There are parts of this story I cannot discuss.”
“A situation near me made visibility feel risky.”
“People and systems I depended on were no longer available.”
“The infrastructure changed underneath the business.”

## True-story card
Suggested:
“Inspired by true events and personal experiences. Certain scenes, timelines,
characters, and visual details have been condensed or dramatized for storytelling.
No dramatization should be understood as an allegation of criminal conduct.”
