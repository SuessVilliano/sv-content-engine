# Distribution Plan

## Canonical source
YouTube long-form is the main story archive.

## Trailer launch
T-7 days: 8-second visual teaser, no explanation.
T-3 days: 15-second cut.
T-1 day: still image + “Tomorrow.”
Launch: 60-second master on YouTube + 30-second vertical cut.
T+1: one story lesson from trailer.
T+3: behind-the-scenes character/world build.
T+5: Episode 001 date/title reveal.

## Rule
Social media receives trailers, artifacts and lessons.
It does not receive unlimited access to Jamaur.

## Content hierarchy
Long-form story -> searchable education -> shorts -> social excerpts -> email/community.
