# Voiceover Direction

Use Jamaur's real voice if possible.

Performance:
- low-key, intimate, close-mic
- speak to one person, not an audience
- no motivational-speaker cadence
- allow breath and silence
- underplay the hardest lines
- do not sound angry when discussing losses
- certainty should increase only in final third

Record two versions:
A. restrained / documentary
B. slightly more cinematic

Recommended recording:
- 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV
- dry recording, minimal room reflections
- pop filter
- 6–8 inches from microphone
- capture 30 seconds of room tone
