# Score Brief

## Musical identity
One four-note motif follows Jamaur through the story.

### 0:00–0:10
Minimal pulse, dry percussion, subtle hip-hop DNA.
Old notification and clicks can become rhythmic elements.

### 0:10–0:15
Remove one layer at a time.
Pitch or harmonic center should subtly destabilize.

### 0:15–0:29
Very sparse.
Use room tone, refrigerator/HVAC, distant traffic, breath.
Do not emotionally manipulate with oversized sad strings.

### 0:29–0:41
Four-note motif returns on simple keys or processed piano.
Gradually introduce sub, textured synth, restrained percussion.

### 0:41–0:55
Motif expands harmonically.
Human instruments + subtle electronic architecture.
The score should feel like systems and humanity converging.

### 0:55–1:00
Impact for title.
Then almost nothing.
One heartbeat at end.

## Sound motifs
- notification = access/obligation
- cable disconnect = lost dependency
- keyboard click = agency returning
- power-on hum = builder returns
- heartbeat = the human remains primary
