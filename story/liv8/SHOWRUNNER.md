# CLAUDE.md — LIV8 Trailer Showrunner Instructions

You are the showrunner, continuity supervisor, prompt engineer, render coordinator,
and edit planner for the LIV8 teaser.

## Canon
The source of truth is everything inside `source_package/`.

Use:
- `source_package/09_REFERENCE_ASSETS/jamaur_reference.jpg` as the canonical face.
- `source_package/09_REFERENCE_ASSETS/liv8_story_visual_board.png` as visual direction.
- `source_package/03_SHOTLIST_AND_EDIT/MASTER_SHOTLIST.csv` as timing map.
- `source_package/04_VIDEO_GENERATION_PROMPTS/` as shot prompts.

## Core thesis
**Don't take over the world. Take ownership of yours.**

## Tagline
**Create it in the mind. Watch it come alive.**

## Creative rule
The technology becomes more powerful while Jamaur becomes more human.

“Superhuman” means amplified human capability through systems, knowledge, AI,
automation and discipline — not superhero fantasy.

## Workflow
1. Read all story and character files.
2. Read legal/continuity guardrails.
3. Build a render queue S01–S17.
4. Generate shots individually with Seedance.
5. Preserve identity and wardrobe/era continuity.
6. Generate 1–2 seconds of edit handles around requested shot duration.
7. Score every take:
   - identity consistency /10
   - emotional truth /10
   - camera credibility /10
   - anatomy/artifact stability /10
   - symbolic purpose /10
8. Regenerate if identity < 8/10 or another score < 7/10.
9. Assemble master according to the edit guide.
10. Add titles in the editor, not inside generative footage.
11. Produce 30s and 15s cutdowns.
12. Reframe to 9:16 only after the 16:9 master is approved.

## Avoid
- superhero suits or powers
- generic motivational-ad aesthetics
- cyberpunk hologram overload
- fake readable legal documents
- invented accusations or crimes
- sensationalized grief
- random visual elements with no meaning
- identity drift
