# LIV8 / HUSTLE. PRAY. STAY OUT THE WAY.
## Teaser Trailer Production Package — v1.0

This folder is the complete pre-production handoff for a cinematic, stylized,
true-story teaser trailer built around Jamaur's LIV8 universe.

### Core idea
**Don't take over the world. Take ownership of yours.**

The protagonist is not a superhero. He becomes a **superhuman** by combining
experience, systems, AI, automation, finance, health, family, and consciousness
to reclaim time and direct his own life.

### Trailer target
- Master: 60 seconds, 16:9, 4K
- Cutdowns: 30 sec and 15 sec
- Social reframes: 9:16 safe-center composition
- Visual style: cinematic graphic-novel realism / stylized true-story animation
- Narrator: Jamaur's real voice
- On-screen Jamaur: animated/stylized likeness based on the supplied reference image
- Tone: mysterious, grounded, emotionally intelligent, never self-congratulatory

### Included
1. Master teaser screenplay
2. Story and symbolism bible
3. Canonical character bible
4. Timecoded shot list and EDL
5. Per-shot generation prompts
6. Audio, score, and sound-design brief
7. 30-second and 15-second cutdowns
8. YouTube/social launch copy
9. Legal and factual-storytelling guardrails
10. Reference imagery and asset manifest
11. Render/handoff instructions

### Important
This package is designed to be rendered in a video-generation/editing stack
such as Higgsfield, Seedance, Veo, Runway, Kling, Premiere, Resolve, or similar.
The final motion-video render itself is not included because this chat does not
have a native video renderer.
