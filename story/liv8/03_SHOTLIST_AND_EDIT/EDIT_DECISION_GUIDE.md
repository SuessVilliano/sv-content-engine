# Edit Decision Guide

## Rhythm
0:00–0:10: fast, 1–2 second cuts. Seductive momentum.
0:10–0:23: slow down progressively. Let shots breathe.
0:23–0:29: nearly still. Social disappearance.
0:29–0:46: controlled acceleration. Not frantic.
0:46–0:55: emotional resolution through longer holds.
0:55–1:00: graphic impact -> silence.

## Transition language
- Early years: whip pans, motivated match cuts, foreground wipes
- Floor-moves era: match cuts that break symmetry
- Collapse: hard cuts and locked frames, no flashy transitions
- Underground: reflections, occlusion, darkness-to-darkness
- Return: power-on transitions, monitor glow, precise push-ins
- AI: object morphs and causal transformations
- LIV8 reveal: typography breaks apart and re-assembles

## Composition
Always protect center 45% of frame for future 9:16 crops.
Do not place essential story information only at extreme horizontal edges.

## Grade
Hustle: warmer artificial highlights, higher contrast, nightlife energy.
Collapse: desaturated neutrals, controlled blacks, fewer highlights.
Underground: sodium-vapor street ambience / dark neutral interiors.
Return: cleaner blacks, restrained practicals.
Present/future: natural light and wider tonal range.

## Performance editing
Prioritize micro-expression, hands, pauses, looking away, turning off devices,
and physical stillness over exaggerated reaction shots.
